| D-B3 | 2026-10-30 | Frustration F = sum of |w| over edges violated by a two-camp partition. Heuristic: spectral start, plus 9 simulated-annealing starts (work budget ~2e6 node-visits per restart, so 100-5000 sweeps), each polished by greedy single-node flips; best of 10 restarts. Validated against an exact ILP (scipy HiGHS) on 16 random-sign graphs of 36-77 nodes (hardest, null-like case): optimal in 14/16 (88%), mean relative gap 0.08%, max 1.2%; the heuristic never beats a proved optimum (tested). Gap at 40k+ nodes is NOT verified (ILP cannot solve those). An earlier annealing budget (100 sweeps) gave 38% optimal and a 5% mean gap, so the budget was raised before use. | greedy-only (gap ~5%) | Same heuristic scores observed and null graphs, so residual gap partly cancels in P_B but not exactly; revisit if a gap at scale matters. | open |
| D-B4 | 2026-10-30 | P_B = 1 - F_obs / F_null; F_null = mean frustration over R sign-shuffled copies of the same topology (edge weights permuted among edges). P_B = 0 when F_null = 0 (e.g. all-positive; tree-like graphs have no cycles to frustrate). Secondary null: signed-degree-preserving rewiring (double edge swaps inside positive and negative edge sets separately; 10 swaps per edge), reported as P_B_rewire. R: 50 sign-shuffle draws and 10 rewires for continuous_llm, 20 and 0 for the other sources. `TODO(verify)` the exact normalisation in Guo et al. Raw F, F_null mean/sd and total weight A are stored. | | Per plan. Cost is small (see Phase 3 report). | open |
